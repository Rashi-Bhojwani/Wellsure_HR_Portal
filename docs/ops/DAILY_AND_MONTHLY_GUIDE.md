# Daily and monthly guide

**For:** HR, managers, employees and the two directors. Screen names are exactly as they appear in the menu.

---

## HR, each day

**Employees**
1. **Employee → All Employees**: find anyone; open a profile to change details. Job changes take an effective date.
2. **Joiners**: **Employee → Add Employee**. Then **Employee → Onboarding**: press **Start** for the new joiner. The joiner and their manager are told which steps are theirs.
3. **Change Requests** (Employee → Change Requests):
   - Approve or reject what employees asked to change.
   - Bank and statutory changes that HR entered wait here for a director, under "Bank and statutory changes waiting for a director". You can withdraw your own entry while it waits.
4. **Probation** (Employee → Probation): people whose probation ends soon or has passed. **Confirm**, or **Extend** to a later date. Each needs a reason, and the employee sees it.
5. **Leavers**: **Employee → Separations**.
6. **Letters** (Employee → Letters): prepare letters. Employees can ask for a letter from Document Center → Ask for a letter, once a director has listed which letters may be asked for.
7. **Company Documents**: publish a policy. To ask everyone it is published to to read it and acknowledge it, use the acknowledgement icon on the document's row.
8. **Helpdesk** and **Employee Feedback**: answer tickets, and answer feedback that was sent with a name.

**Attendance** (Workforce Management → Attendance)
1. **Who Is In?** shows today, live.
2. **Attendance Exception** shows days that need a look, such as a missing punch.
3. **Regularizations**: decide employees' correction requests. Nobody decides their own.
4. **Process Attendance** recomputes days after a fix.
5. **Attendance Muster**: branches without a machine can be marked here, or a filled-in muster uploaded (Upload muster).

**Leave** (Workforce Management → Leave)
1. **Employee Leave**: requests waiting, approve or reject.
2. **Leave Granter**: grant (or take back) leave days, with remarks.

## Managers

Managers start with no permissions. HR or a director grants each one what they need.

1. **To do → Review**: approve or reject your team's leave and attendance corrections (once granted).
2. **To do → Onboarding**: the steps you own for a new joiner in your team.
3. **To do → Tasks**: everything waiting on you.

## Employees

1. **Leave → Leave Apply**, **Leave Balances**, **Holiday Calendar**.
2. **Attendance → Attendance Info**: your month. Click a day to ask for a correction.
3. **Document Center**: company policies (acknowledge them when asked), your letters, and **Ask for a letter**.
4. **Salary → Payslips** and **Form 16**: your own only.
5. **My Worklife → Feedback**: tell HR something, with your name or without it.
6. **Request Hub**: everything you asked for, in one place.

## The directors, each month

Before payroll, **HR finalises the month's attendance**: **Workforce Management → Attendance → Attendance Period Finalisation**. Payroll pays only for finalised days.

1. **Payroll → Monthly Inputs**: the month's one-off figures. It opens when attendance is final. The other director confirms.
2. **Payroll → Payroll Run → Start a payroll run** and choose the month.
3. **Calculate**. Read "Left out" (who is not paid, and why), the totals, and **Difference from last month**.
4. **Submit for approval** (the step page is "Submit the run for approval"). It shows everyone left out, first.
5. **Approval**: the other director, if you chose "a different director must decide" for this step. A change after approval voids it.
6. **Close the period** ("Close the payroll period"). The month is then locked: late corrections go into the next month as **Adjustments**.
7. **Payslips and bank file**:
   - **Publish payslips**: the top of the page names everyone who will get no payslip, and why.
   - **Generate the bank file**: made in your confirmed layout.
8. **Upload the bank file to your bank's website yourself.** The portal never moves money. Then, on the run:
   - **Mark sent to the bank**;
   - **Record that the bank paid this batch**;
   - **Enter rejected payments** by hand, if any (there is no file back from the bank).
9. **Statutory files**: PF, ESI, PT and LWF files from the final run, once each layout is confirmed. Check each one on the government portal before filing.
10. **Payroll → Statutory challans**: record each deposit. The other director confirms it by typing the amount and reference again.
11. **Payroll → TDS and Form 16**: deducted against deposited, month by month, and the Form 16 data. Form 16 stays a DRAFT.
12. **Payroll → Compliance calendar**, and **Payroll → Payroll reports**.

Other payroll screens you will use:
- **Held Salaries**, **Not on Payroll**, **Adjustments**, **Off-cycle runs**, **Settlements** (leavers).
- **Loans**, **Reimbursements**.
- **Overtime and encashment pay**, which pays approved overtime and encashment once those settings are turned on.

Every screen that decides something records who did it and when (**Admin Tools → Audit Log**). Exports of reports and of the audit log are recorded too.

## "It won't let me": what the message means

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "You don't have access" | Your role does not include this screen. | Ask a director. HR never sees pay screens, by design. |
| "The two directors have not both chosen how … is decided" | Director authority is not set for that step. | System Settings → Director authority: one sets it, the other confirms. |
| "This cannot be submitted while a rule it used is still a Placeholder: …" | The run is a TEST run. | Have the named item confirmed by the other director, then calculate again. |
| "Attendance is not finalised for N of their days …" | Payroll pays only for finalised days. | Finalise the month's attendance first. |
| "Something changed since you looked …" | A figure moved after you opened the preview. | Look again, then repeat the step. This is the safety working. |
| "The figures changed after they were submitted, so this approval is void …" | Something the approval covered changed. | Submit again. |
| "You proposed this, so a different director confirms it." | The same person cannot propose and confirm. | Ask the other director. |
| "This day's attendance is finalised, so it can't be corrected." | The month is locked. | A late correction goes into next month's payroll as an Adjustment. |
| "You can't decide your own request." | Nobody approves their own leave, correction or claim. | It goes to someone else. |
| "An existing request already covers part of this date range." | Two leave requests overlap. | Cancel or change the earlier one. |
| A bank or PAN change shows "waiting for a director" | HR's bank and statutory entries wait for a director (on by default). | A director confirms it under Employee → Change Requests. |
| "… is not in use" (overtime, work from home, encashment) | The setting is off. | The directors turn it on in Policy settings, if the company wants it. |
| "Not set" | Nobody has chosen this value yet. | The screen says who sets it and where. |
